Questions

Before you book.

Does this replace my doctor?

No. Diagnosis, treatment and medication decisions stay with qualified medical professionals. We help turn their guidance into daily meals and habits.

How are professionals verified?

Each applicant submits a registration number and certificate. Our team checks them with the issuing body before a profile goes live.

How do payments work?

You pay at booking through the platform. The professional is paid after the consultation. Refund and cancellation terms will be shown before you pay [policy to be finalised].

Is my health information private?

Reports and details are shared only with the professionals you book. [Privacy policy and consent text to be finalised with legal counsel.]

I live outside Karachi. Can I still use this?

Video consultations work anywhere. Home and kitchen visits depend on chapter coverage. Tell us your city and we will add you to the list.

What if it is an emergency?

Call your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital. This service is not for emergencies.
